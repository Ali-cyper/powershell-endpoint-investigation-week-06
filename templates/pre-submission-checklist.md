# Pre-submission Checklist

- [ ] Verdict written as TP / FP / UNDETERMINED
- [ ] Reason is positive evidence, not "no damage"
- [ ] Strongest and weakest compared with the normal baseline
- [ ] One-sentence story explains all evidence
- [ ] Missing evidence = things I do not have
- [ ] "What would change my mind" is opposite to my verdict and verifiable
- [ ] Read all SIEM notes
- [ ] Recommendation complete and in order
- [ ] Impact described (not "none")
- [ ] Reviewed the mistake log
